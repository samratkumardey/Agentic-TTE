# Gold annotation for the Phase 0 Replay trials

This is the gold standard for parts B (concepts) and C (TTE elements) of [../SCORING.md](../SCORING.md). Until it exists, no accuracy number can be reported.

## Rules for annotators

1. **Don't open any model output** (`runs/`, `eval/results/`) before your annotation is final and handed in. Work from the registry text in the template only. The `detailed_description` field is included for time zero and causal contrast.
2. Copy `templates/<NCT>.json` to `<NCT>.<your initials>.json` and fill every `null` field and every empty list. Set `annotator` to your initials.
3. **Criteria:** fill `atoms` with the same structure the protocol schema uses (`agentic_tte/protocol/models.py`, `CriterionAtom`). Use the trialist-v2 rules in `agentic_tte/agents/prompts.py` (`CRITERION_SYSTEM`) plus the clarifications in SCORING.md:
   - a concept's `name` is the shortest clinically meaningful noun phrase;
   - expand abbreviations, and add any variant spellings you meet to `synonyms.json`;
   - anchors that mean randomization, enrollment or study entry are `time_zero`.
4. **Registry errors** (for example "Pregnant defined by negative serum HCG"): annotate what the registry says, and describe the error in `registry_error`.
5. **Treatment arms:** `is_comparator`, plus `components` as `TreatmentComponent` (concept, dose, route, frequency, duration, end_event, end_rule). Use `null` when the registry doesn't say.
6. **Time zero:** your preferred `anchor_event` in plain words, plus `acceptable_alternatives`: other anchors a reasonable trialist could defend. Reviewers judge model anchors against both.
7. **Follow-up and primary outcome:** horizon and end event, as in the registry time frame. `_parser_hint_not_gold` shows what our parser reads. It is not gold; overrule it whenever it's wrong.
8. **Causal contrast:** `value` (`intention_to_treat` or `per_protocol`), plus `origin` `registry` with the quoted `evidence` when the record states it. Otherwise `origin` is `default`.

Two annotators fill every trial independently. A third adjudicates disagreements into `<NCT>.gold.json`. Report Cohen's κ on concept presence plus domain before adjudication.

## Files

- `templates/`: generated by `eval/make_gold_templates.py` from the cached registry records (public data). Regenerate them only if the extractor changes, and note it in the SCORING.md changelog.
- `synonyms.json`: adjudicated `{variant: canonical}` concept names, used by `eval/phase0_scoring.py`.
- `<NCT>.<initials>.json`, `<NCT>.gold.json`: annotations.
