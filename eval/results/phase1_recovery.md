## Notes

- EmulatRx's synthetic estimates (last column) are attenuated toward HR 1. Two of our findings bear on that: their Cox model used a ridge penalty (`penalizer=0.1`, here "upstream ridge"), and their comparison used the planted conditional HR as truth, while IPTW and PSM estimate a marginal HR that is genuinely closer to 1. Their data and seed differ from ours, so the columns are not directly comparable.
- Clone-censor-weight estimates the grace-period strategy "start at the natural time if you would have started, otherwise at the deadline". "Everyone starts at a random time within the grace period" is a different strategy; both truths are in `agentic_tte/synth/dgp.py`.
- CIs for weighted Cox models are robust (sandwich). CCW CIs are clustered on the patient, but do not account for estimating the weights; bootstrap them for reporting.
- **One sample per row, shared across rows.** All point-treatment scenarios use the same seed, so the same patients, covariates and treatment assignment; their estimation errors are correlated. The consistent small upward deviation of IPTW and PSM (PSM about +0.06 on the log scale) is therefore one observation, not four. Bias and coverage claims need a Monte Carlo study over many seeds (planned).
